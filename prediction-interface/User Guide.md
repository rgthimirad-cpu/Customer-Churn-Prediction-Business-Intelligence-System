### **USER GUIDE**

##### **Customer Churn Prediction System**



**Introduction**



The Customer Churn Prediction System is designed to help business users identify customers who are likely to discontinue their services.



The system uses a trained machine learning model to analyse customer information and provide:



\- Churn Probability

\- Risk Classification

\- Churn Risk Factors

\- Retention Recommendations



This information can support customer retention strategies and business decision-making.







**Application Workflow**



1\. Enter customer details.

2\. Click Predict Churn.

3\. Review prediction results.

4\. Analyse risk factors.

5\. Apply recommended retention actions.







**Customer Information Section**



Users must enter customer profile information.



Input Fields



| Field | Description |

|---------|-------------|

| Senior Citizen | Indicates whether customer is a senior citizen |

| Partner | Indicates whether customer has a partner |

| Dependents | Indicates whether customer has dependents |

| Tenure Months | Number of months the customer has used the service |







**Service Information Section**



Users must provide service subscription details.



Input Fields



| Field | Description |

|---------|-------------|

| Multiple Lines | Customer uses multiple phone lines |

| Internet Service | Type of internet service |

| Online Security | Online security subscription |

| Online Backup | Online backup subscription |

| Device Protection | Device protection service |

| Tech Support | Technical support subscription |

| Streaming TV | Streaming television service |

| Streaming Movies | Streaming movie service |







**Billing Information Section**



Users must provide billing and payment information.



Input Fields



| Field | Description |

|---------|-------------|

| Contract | Contract type |

| Paperless Billing | Paperless billing preference |

| Payment Method | Customer payment method |

| Monthly Charges | Monthly service cost |

| Total Charges | Total accumulated charges |







**Generating a Prediction**



After completing all required fields:



1\. Click the \*\*Predict Churn\*\* button.

2\. The machine learning model will process the customer information.

3\. Prediction results will be displayed instantly.





**Understanding Prediction Results**



Churn Probability



Displays the likelihood of customer churn as a percentage.



Example:



84.70%

Higher percentages indicate a greater probability of churn.



Risk Level

The system categorises customers into three risk groups.

LOW Risk

0% - 30%

Customer is unlikely to churn.

MEDIUM Risk

31% - 60%

Customer may require monitoring and engagement.

HIGH Risk

Above 60%

Customer is likely to churn and may require immediate retention action.



**Top Churn Drivers**

The application highlights key factors that contribute to customer churn risk.

Example:

•	Short tenure

•	Month-to-month contract

•	High monthly charges

These factors help users understand why a customer is considered at risk.



**Retention Recommendations**

The system recommends actions based on the customer's risk level.



**HIGH Risk Customers**

Recommended actions:

•	Personalised retention offers

•	Dedicated support assistance

•	Immediate customer engagement



**MEDIUM Risk Customers**

Recommended actions:

•	Loyalty incentives

•	Promotional offers

•	Customer engagement campaigns



**LOW Risk Customers**

Recommended actions:

•	Maintain regular communication

•	Continue delivering quality service

•	Monitor customer satisfaction

**Purpose of the System**

The Customer Churn Prediction System assists organisations in:

•	Identifying at-risk customers

•	Understanding churn drivers

•	Improving customer retention

•	Supporting business decision-making
